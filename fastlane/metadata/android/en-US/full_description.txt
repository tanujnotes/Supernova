<p>Supernova is a home screen you can shape to fit the way you use your phone. It works fully offline: no account, no tracking, no ads and no internet permission.</p>

<p><b>Home screen and dock</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Adjustable grid, icon size, labels and margins, and a dock with multiple pages</li>
<li>Widgets with resizing, padding and stacking</li>
<li>Multiple pages with scroll effects and a page indicator</li>
<li>Folders with custom previews, backgrounds and swipe-to-open</li>
</ul>

<p><b>App drawer</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Vertical grid, list or horizontal pages, with a fast scroller</li>
<li>Tabs and folders to group apps, a separate Work tab and hidden apps</li>
<li>Recently opened apps row and instant search for apps, contacts and calculations</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Look and feel</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Icon packs, custom icons and icon shapes, including themed icons</li>
<li>Notification badges in several styles (needs notification access)</li>
<li>Light, dark and automatic night mode</li>
<li>Launch, drawer and page transition animations</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Gestures</b></p>
<p>Swipe, double tap, pinch and two-finger gestures can open apps, shortcuts, the drawer, notifications, search or lock the screen.</p>

<p><b>Feed and cards</b></p>
<p>An optional page beside the home screen with your calendar, media controls, frequently used apps and your own cards. Cards hold apps, shortcuts or a widget, and can show up automatically when conditions such as time or a Bluetooth device are met.</p>

<p><b>Backup</b></p>
<p>Export and restore your whole setup, including layout and settings, to a file.</p>

<p><b>Permissions</b></p>
<p>Every permission is optional and only used for the feature that asks for it: contacts for search and the feed, calendar for the calendar card, nearby devices for Bluetooth card conditions, notification access for badges and the media card, and the accessibility service or device admin for gestures that lock the screen or open recents and quick settings.</p>
